# Payment and Billing Terms

Beta information effective/updated: 13 September 2026. Version 2026-09-13-beta-2.

## 1. Price and quote

Plans are displayed in GBP. Before payment, the server records the normal price, discount, final GBP amount and USD charge, using a dated ECB reference rate through Frankfurter. The quote lasts 15 minutes. Daily reference rates are not live trading rates. Review any additional payment-service or bank conversion fees before confirming payment.

## 2. Payment verification

Lane uses PayGate and a specifically selected payment route. Identity verification may be required by the payment service. A return page is not proof of payment. Access begins only after server verification. Incorrect, late or ambiguous amounts require review; do not pay again while a payment is being reconciled.

## 3. Manual monthly renewal

One verified payment grants one calendar month. Early same-plan renewal extends the existing expiration. After expiry, the month starts from verified payment completion. Calculations use UTC and the last valid day where the next month is shorter. An active plan change requires review. No automatic recurring charge or saved-card mandate is created.

## 4. Discounts and expiry

Discounts may be account-, plan-, environment-, time- or use-limited. The final amount must meet the current payment minimum. Only the server-created order controls price and access. Expiry locks paid publishing, not sign-in, export, history, support or deletion requests. Data is retained and a verified renewal restores eligible access.

## 5. Cancellation and refunds

Not renewing prevents another monthly purchase. This is separate from your statutory cancellation and remedy rights. Lane's additional three-day refund offer and contact process are described in the Cancellation and Refund Policy. There is no automatic refund system. The checkout agreement does not waive statutory rights or obtain a digital-content cancellation waiver.
