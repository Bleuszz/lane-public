# Account and data requests

Beta information effective/updated: 13 September 2026. Version 2026-09-13-beta-2.

## 1. Your choices

Account management offers inventory CSV export and a deletion-request action, including after paid access expires. A request creates a support record; it does not instantly delete data. Contact support for access, correction, restriction, objection or other privacy requests.

## 2. Verification and retention

Lane may need proportionate identity verification before changing or disclosing an account. Do not send passwords or identity documents unsolicited. Any lawful retention exception will be explained. Erasure rights are not absolute; financial records and disputes may have separate retention requirements. Operator-specific retention periods are not yet finalised.

## 3. Desktop and marketplaces

Revoke a device through Lane's website to remove its cloud access. Disconnect marketplace accounts in Desktop to remove its saved local sessions. Uninstall preserves app data in the Windows beta. Deleting Lane data does not delete a listing on a marketplace.
