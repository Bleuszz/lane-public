# Data and privacy

Your Lane account is separate from your marketplace accounts. Account, inventory metadata and device information are stored online. Listing photographs may be links to marketplace images; this is not a promise of permanent photograph storage. Keep your originals.

Marketplace passwords are entered on the marketplace's own page. Lane's desktop workflow retains local marketplace session data. Disconnect the marketplace in Lane to remove its local copy. Revoking a desktop device in your Lane account removes that device's access to Lane; it does not close your marketplace account.

Expiry of a trial or paid period does not delete your inventory or account history.

For account, export or deletion help, use [Lane account](https://lane-staging.onrender.com/account) and [Contact](https://lane-staging.onrender.com/contact). Support availability is shown there. Requesting deletion is not confirmation that deletion has been completed. Do not post private information in GitHub issues.

Read the [beta privacy notice](https://lane-staging.onrender.com/privacy) and [terms](https://lane-staging.onrender.com/terms). Commercial launch remains subject to completion of the operator's legal and contact information. No certifications or independent audit are claimed.
