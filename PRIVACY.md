# Privacy Notice

Beta information effective/updated: 13 September 2026. Version 2026-09-13-beta-2.

## 1. Account and service information

Lane stores account identity and email, protected authentication credentials, sessions, trial and access dates, device authorisations, inventory data, photo references and service activity. These support account administration and the requested service. Payment orders record prices, exchange rates, discounts, document acceptance and payment references. Lane does not collect card details or wallet private keys.

## 2. Marketplace sessions

Desktop marketplace cookies stay local under Windows-protected encryption in the current session flow. Lane account device tokens are separate and revocable. Cloud device health contains limited connection and version information, not raw marketplace cookies. External photo URLs can expire or change; permanent photo storage is not currently promised.

## 3. Support and providers

Support stores your account reference, topic, message, reply email and receipt. Database-backed requests remain saved if email is disabled. Render hosts the application and Neon stores its PostgreSQL data, using the configured EU deployment. GitHub distributes public desktop files. PayGate is used when an approved payment is initiated and receives necessary payment-routing information. Resend notification delivery and Google sign-in are optional and currently unconfigured. No live AI provider receives your data.

## 4. Purposes and choices

Account and requested-service processing is needed to provide the service; proportionate security processing protects accounts. Consent is required before optional measurement where applicable. No marketing analytics or advertising pixels are enabled in this staging deployment. The operator's detailed lawful-basis, transfer and retention schedule must be finalised before commercial payment launch.

## 5. Retention and rights

Expiry does not erase your records. You can request access, correction, export or deletion, and raise objections or restrictions where applicable. Deletion is reviewed and is not an immediate automatic wipe; records may need retention for legal obligations or disputes. Specific category retention periods remain to be set by the operator. You may complain to the ICO if concerned about the handling of personal data.
